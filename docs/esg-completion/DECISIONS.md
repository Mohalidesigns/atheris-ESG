| D22 | Carbon engine, Scope 2/3 methods, flaring, targets and PCAF (WP-21) | **DECIDED** — architect (Claude), WP-21, 1 Oct 2026; method choices, constants and factor values **for ESG SME sign-off** | (1) **Per-gas CO2e** (TRD §8.2): CO2e t = activity × (EF_CO2 + EF_CH4 × GWP_CH4 + EF_N2O × GWP_N2O) ÷ 1000. Changing it affects new calculations; existing records change only when recalculated. (2) **Provenance on every record**: factor id, version, value, unit, level (selected/custom/country/region/global), GWP set and per-gas tonnes are stored; published factor values stay immutable (WP-17). The client never sends co2eTonnes. (3) **Recalculation** is the TRD's `POST /api/v1/carbon/calculate` ("trigger emission calculation job"): for a date range it re-resolves the factor and GWP set of every unverified, factor-based record whose factor wasn't chosen explicitly and updates those that change, with a lineage row. (5) **Scope 2 market-based** (GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance): RECs/PPAs/green tariffs are matched to grid-electricity records of the same organisation with overlapping periods; avoided = min(instrument MWh × 1000, matched kWh) × (average matched grid factor − instrument factor) ÷ 1000; market = location − avoided. Simplifications for SME review: no residual-mix factors (uncovered kWh keep the grid factor); overlap is by whole record, not pro-rated by days; two instruments over the same records can each claim the same kWh — not yet detected (open, see STATUS WP-21). Hybrid and Cat 3 well-to-tank factors are not built. Scope 3 records must carry their category (1–15). Intensity targets use the denominators in (9). (9) **Intensity** (BRD-5.2.2-07): per million base-currency revenue, per employee and per unit produced, from the year's approved `revenue`, `employees_total` and `production_output` data points; nothing is shown when no denominator is approved.
