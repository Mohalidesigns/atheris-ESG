| D22 | Carbon engine, Scope 2/3 methods, flaring, targets and PCAF (WP-21) | **DECIDED** — architect (Claude), WP-21, 1 Oct 2026; method choices, constants and factor values **for ESG SME sign-off** | (1) **Per-gas CO2e** (TRD §8.2): CO2e t = activity × (EF_CO2 + EF_CH4 × GWP_CH4 + EF_N2O × GWP_N2O) ÷ 1000. GWP sets in `reference.gwp_values`: AR5 (CH4 28, N2O 265) and AR6 (CH4 fossil 29.8, N2O 273); tenant setting `gwp_set` defaults to **AR6** (current IPCC; GHG Protocol accepts it). Changing it affects new calculations; existing records change only when recalculated. (2) **Provenance on every record**: factor id, version, value, unit, level (selected/custom/country/region/global), GWP set and per-gas tonnes are stored; published factor values stay immutable (WP-17). The client never sends co2eTonnes. (3) **Recalculation** is the TRD's `POST /api/v1/carbon/calculate` ("trigger emission calculation job"): for a date range it re-resolves the factor and GWP set of every unverified, factor-based record whose factor wasn't chosen explicitly and updates those that change, with a lineage row. Verified and flaring records are left alone. `POST /carbon/emissions/preview` (not in the TRD catalogue) returns the calculator's result without saving, so the wizard shows exactly what will be stored. (4) **Data Hub → carbon**: when an activity metric (`reference.metric_activities`: electricity_kwh, diesel_litres, petrol_litres, lpg_kg, natural_gas_m3) is approved, an emission record is created with lineage to the data point; reopening the point deletes it; such records can't be edited directly. (5) **Scope 2 market-based** (GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance): RECs/PPAs/green tariffs are matched to grid-electricity records of the same organisation with overlapping periods; avoided = min(instrument MWh × 1000, matched kWh) × (average matched grid factor − instrument factor) ÷ 1000; market = location − avoided. Simplifications for SME review: no residual-mix factors (uncovered kWh keep the grid factor); overlap is by whole record, not pro-rated by days; two instruments over the same records can each claim the same kWh — not yet detected (open, see STATUS WP-21). (6) **Scope 3**: activity-based with any factor (e.g. Cat 6 air travel); spend-based only with the tenant's own spend factors (`factor_method = spend`, kgCO2e per currency unit) — no EEIO dataset is loaded (licence/SME), and spend in another currency is converted with the tenant's FX rate (D21 (6)). Hybrid and Cat 3 well-to-tank factors are not built. Scope 3 records must carry their category (1–15). (7) **Gas flaring** (API Compendium 2021 combustion stoichiometry): n = V (Mcf) × 1000 ÷ 379.3 scf/lbmol; CO2 = n × [Σ yᵢ Cᵢ × η + y_CO2] × 44.01 lb; CH4 slip = n × y_CH4 × (1 − η) × 16.043 lb; 0.45359237 kg/lb; C5+ counted as 5 carbons; N2O omitted (negligible for flares); η defaults to **0.98**; composition must total 100 ± 0.5 mole % and use known components. Intensity targets use the denominators in (9). (9) **Intensity** (BRD-5.2.2-07): per million base-currency revenue, per employee and per unit produced, from the year's approved `revenue`, `employees_total` and `production_output` data points; nothing is shown when no denominator is approved. Counterparty emissions are entered, not fetched. (11) **Endpoints beyond the TRD catalogue** (TRD lists calculate, flaring, targets): instruments, financed (+ summary), emissions preview and verify, flaring satellite-check, gwp — all under `/api/v1/carbon`. | WP-21 |
